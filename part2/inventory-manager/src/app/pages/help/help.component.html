Help & User Guide

Quick guide to using the Inventory Management System.

Adding Items

Fill in all required fields marked with (*). Item ID must be unique. Click "Add Item" to save.

Loading Items

Enter the item name and click "Load Item" to fill the form for editing.

Editing Items

Load an item first, modify the fields, then click "Save Edit" to update changes.

Deleting Items

Enter the full item name and click "Delete Item" to remove it permanently.

Search & Filter

Use the Search page to find items by name or filter by category.
